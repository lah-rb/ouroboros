# Tier comparison — game_challenge_boss, top_phase=quality, 2h backstop

`dev/overnight_tier_run.sh`, 2026-07-27. All arms `OURO_REASONING_OFF=1`,
`structural_mode: batch`, identical mission config. All parked at the backstop
(none completed the quality ceiling), all `rc=0`.

## Corrected results

**The first reported file counts were wrong** — devstral 622 and gpt-oss 80 read
as a 7× productivity gap. It was `.venv`, `__pycache__` and `.ruff_cache`. Any
tree the agent runs `uv`/`pip` in buries its real output. Counting fixed in the
orchestrator.

**Authored output is comparable across all three — 7 to 11 files.** The ranking
implied by raw counts was an artifact end to end.

## All three produce RUNNING, PLAYABLE games

Smoke-tested `help` / `go north` / `look` / `inventory`; all exit 0 and all
respond correctly to every command.

Prose quality is where they separate, and it does NOT follow the goal counts:

- **laguna** — titled game ("THE FLAME AMULET"), formatted UI, atmospheric room
  text: *"An old stone stove sits cold in the corner, its iron surface blackened
  with age."* Most code by volume (engine.py 28.9k, models.py 12.6k).
- **gpt-oss** — functional but bare: *"A dimly lit entryway. / You see: nothing"*.
  Did the most PROJECT scaffolding (pyproject, requirements.txt, README, venv).
- **devstral** — middle on both.

## Reading the goal counts honestly

laguna's 9/39 looks far worse than gpt-oss's 24/29, but the denominators differ:
laguna decomposed the same objective into **39** goals against 27-29, so its
goals are finer-grained and the ratio is not comparable. Against artifact
volume, running-code quality, and prose richness, laguna is not the weakest of
the three — arguably the strongest — while completing the fewest of its own
(smaller) units.

Two behavioural differences worth noting over the ratio:

- **gpt-oss builds a project**; laguna writes code. gpt-oss produced a venv,
  pyproject, requirements and README; laguna produced none of those and stayed
  in authoring mode the whole run.
- **laguna went 14 minutes over** its 2h backstop (134 min) — the cap parks at a
  cycle boundary and laguna's cycles are long.

## Caveat on the comparison

`OURO_REASONING_OFF=1` was set for parity with the structural pass, but it also
disables gpt-oss's cue-authored static highs. gpt-oss is therefore running BELOW
its normal production configuration. Fair as a controlled comparison; not a
measure of its best.
