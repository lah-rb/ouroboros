## Reading the goal counts honestly

laguna's 9/39 looks far worse than gpt-oss's 24/29, but the denominators differ:
laguna decomposed the same objective into **39** goals against 27-29, so its
goals are finer-grained and the ratio is not comparable. Against artifact
volume, running-code quality, and prose richness, laguna is not the weakest of
the three — arguably the strongest — while completing the fewest of its own
(smaller) units.

Two behavioural differences worth noting over the ratio:

- **gpt-oss builds a project**; laguna writes code. gpt-oss produced a venv,
  pyproject, requirements and README; laguna produced none of those and stayed
  in authoring mode the whole run.
- **laguna went 14 minutes over** its 2h backstop (134 min) — the cap parks at a
  cycle boundary and laguna's cycles are long.

## Where the run time actually went (Luke's hypothesis, confirmed)

Parsed from the health-watchdog ticks, which fire every 30s *inside a single
generation*. A generation is bounded by a drop in the cumulative token count.

| model | long generations | time in them | hit the token cap | cost of the capped ones |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **laguna** | 13 | **69 min of 134 (51%)** | 2 (32,579 / 32,667) | **39 min = 29% of the run** |
| gpt-oss | 4 | 9 min of 120 (8%) | **0** | 0 |
| devstral-2 | 2 | 36 min of 120 (30%) | 1 (48,903) | 33 min = 27% of the run |

**This substantially explains the goal counts.** laguna spent over half its run
inside long generations and lost 39 minutes to two that hit the cap and returned
truncated, unusable output. gpt-oss lost essentially nothing — 8% in long
generations, zero capped — which is most of why it completed 24/29.

So laguna's 9/39 is a **throughput and termination** story before it is a
capability story. Recover those two capped generations and ~29% of the run comes
back.

Note devstral's cap is `max_tokens_default: 131072` against 32768 for the other
two, so its single 48,903-token generation never hit a cap — it ran 33 minutes
and terminated on its own. Different failure mode: not truncation, just an
enormous answer.

**This raises the value of the queued temperature sweep and of `reasoning_budget`
(f7ccbbe):** both target exactly the generations that are eating the runs.

## Caveat on the comparison

`OURO_REASONING_OFF=1` was set for parity with the structural pass, but it also
disables gpt-oss's cue-authored static highs. gpt-oss is therefore running BELOW
its normal production configuration. Fair as a controlled comparison; not a
measure of its best.
